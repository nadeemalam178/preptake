## 4. Compliance, Copyright & Provenance Protocol

1. **Official Portals (CBSE NIC / BPSC / BSEB)**: Public archives provide authentic examination booklets and master answer keys. Where CLI requests timeout due to NIC security firewalls, files downloaded from official candidate portals are processed offline via `content-pipeline/downloads/`.
2. **Coaching Portals (Adda247 / Testbook)**: Only public question paper uploads and free practice resources are downloaded. Paywalls, subscriber test-series, and CAPTCHAs are never bypassed.
3. **Attribution & Classification Rule**:
   - `official_pyq_verified`: Independently cross-checked against authentic question paper and official master answer key.
   - `coaching_pyq_unverified`: Uploaded by coaching platforms as PYQs without official master key verification.
   - `coaching_practice`: Practice questions from coaching resources.
   - `original_practice`: Newly authored, syllabus-aligned questions across NCERT & state syllabus topics.
